About Shnik

A colorful minimalist sliding puzzle game from Amsterdam-based solo developer Little Crown Games. Slide, click, and Shnik your way through 75+ bead maze puzzles.

GAME OVERVIEW

What Is Shnik?

Shnik is a minimalist puzzle game where you manipulate colorful shapes through miniature bead mazes. Your goal in every level is to reunite the red ring with its red center by sliding shapes along rails, rotating track segments, sorting colored pieces, and unlocking barriers.

With its playful vector art, satisfying sound design, and stress-free approach (no timers, no move limits, no fail states), Shnik offers a relaxing yet mentally engaging experience for puzzle fans of all skill levels.

KEY FEATURES

What Makes Shnik Unique

75+ Hand-Crafted Puzzles

Over 75 thoughtfully designed levels that gradually introduce new mechanics, from simple sliding to complex rail rotation and shape sorting.

Unique Sliding Gameplay

A fresh take on the sliding puzzle genre. Manipulate bead mazes by sliding shapes, rotating rails, and unlocking pieces to reunite the red ring with its center.

Colorful Vector Art

Playful, colorful vector art visuals with geometric shapes in soft pastels. A clean, minimalist aesthetic that keeps focus on the puzzle-solving.

Original Soundtrack

A lovely original soundtrack combined with satisfying "tinker-y" sound effects creates a pleasant, immersive puzzling atmosphere.

No Timers, No Pressure

Play at your own pace. No timers, no move limits, and no fail states. Shnik is designed to be a relaxing puzzle experience from start to finish.

Accessibility Features

Color alternatives, contrast controls, mouse-only and touch-only options, and no quick time events make Shnik accessible to a wide range of players.

DEVELOPER

Meet the Creator

Shnik was developed by Little Crown Games, a one-person independent game studio based in Amsterdam, Netherlands. The studio was founded by Geoff Ciccarelli, who handles design, programming, art, and sound for all of his games.

Little Crown Games specializes in small, polished puzzle games with distinctive visual styles. Their previous title, Plungeroo (released October 2023), is a colorful puzzle game about tinkering with mini pinball cabinets. Shnik builds on that same philosophy of accessible, satisfying puzzle design with a fresh bead maze mechanic.
